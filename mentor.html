Mentor Collaboration

Verification & Architectural Guidance from Ms. Johal

1. Defining Standout Systems

When establishing the core development targets for SoulsArena, I consulted with Ms. Johal regarding features to make the capstone stand out. Instead of overloading the engine with shallow assets, she advised prioritizing structural focus over excessive scope expansion.

She highlighted that the success of a combat game hinges directly on how well the enemy AI scales in tandem with the player's progression across consecutive rounds.

  • Implementation: Engineered dynamic behavioral modifiers inside the AI state machines that increase aggressiveness as rounds progress.
  • Design Direction: Shifted development priorities toward deep testing of player onboarding and combat balance mechanics.

2. Polish, Audio & Environment

Following updates on my initial build iteration where characters fought over simple fields, Ms. Johal provided essential feedback detailing specific steps to move the client out of a simple placeholder phase.

Her notes underscored the critical importance of world-building elements—specifically removing basic blue backdrops in favor of custom arena environments, and integrating auditory cues to emphasize player actions.

  • Implementation: Replaced standard background flats with stylized environmental backdrops to support theme consistency.
  • Atmospheric Sound: Added functional sound effect variables tracking key event triggers (combat impacts, jumps, system round wins).
