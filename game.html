Game Development Journey

Click a week to expand and see progress.

Week 1 - Player Movement

In week 1 i worked on my characters movement system. Your basic jumping, going left, right and forward, backward. I also made the the script for the Enemy AI but it doesnt do anything yet.

Week 2 - Free-Flow Combat System start

In Week 2 I started working on the free flow combat system. Its similar to the Batman Arkhem Knight Game Series, i got the character to move between enemies based on the direction you are facing with mouse and/or keyboard.

Week 3/4 - Adding Dynamic Camera and Attack Animations

In Week 3, I added a dynamic camera that changes based on how many enemies there are in the area and started to add attack animations. In Week 4, I added attack and hit animations, the hit reactions will play based on where the enemy is hit, so if u attack from behind, the enemy will get push forward and vice versa for all directions.

Week 5/6 - Attacking Enemies and adding motion warping

I added attack and hit animations using motion warping, Motion Warping in Unreal Engine 5 adjusts animations in real time so a character lines up correctly with a target, like an enemy or ledge, making actions such as attacks or vaults land accurately even if the positioning isn’t perfect.

Week 7 - Adding More Animations and Bug fixing

I added a few more animtions to avoid repetitiveness when attacking and also had a bug where the mid range attack weren't working so I fixed the bug after about 30 minutes of debugging.

Week 8 - Adding Basic AI in the Game

I added Basic AI Functionalty. The enemy just follows the player around and maintains distance.

Week 9-12 - Adding Advanced AI

I added Advanced AI that knows it surroundsings, can hear and see the player, has many attacks and health. I added 2 enemy types. The first is the melee type and the other is ranged type. Finally I added a boss fight at the end of the round to fight and beat.

Week 13 - Final Week - Making World environment and Player UI/Player Model

I made the world in which you the player can interact with and player UI to show health.
